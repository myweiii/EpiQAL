We investigated the persistence of COVID-19-related symptoms in Brazil 4 years after the onset of the pandemic, alongside associated socio-demographic factors, through a nationwide population-based survey. Nearly two-thirds (65.1%) of individuals with a self-reported history of COVID-19 met the criteria for post-COVID-19 condition (PCC), with 44.2% still experiencing symptoms at the time of assessment; among these, women and Indigenous individuals presented a significantly higher average number of symptoms than men or other ethnic groups. Our results document the substantial, ongoing burden of PCC in Brazil, particularly for vulnerable groups, emphasizing the need for targeted public health interventions to address the long-term health sequelae of the pandemic and mitigate health disparities.
