This paper describes the multimorbidity prevalence and incidence trends across socioeconomic groups between 2010 and 2021 by using the “Health Inequalities” cohort dataset—a representative sample covering 22% of the Catalan population in 2005 that is linked to mortality registers. We found evidence of worsening health across the cohorts, with a higher prevalence of multimorbidity in younger cohorts at equivalent ages and a strong socioeconomic gradient. Our findings highlight significant challenges for future healthcare systems, but they also provide evidence that investing in improving individuals’ living conditions and other social determinants of health, through efficient and equitable policies, can have an impact in preventing or delaying the onset of multimorbidity and slowing its progression to more severe stages and death.
