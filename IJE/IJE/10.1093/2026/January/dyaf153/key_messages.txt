We investigated the factors associated with self-reported COVID-19 cases and hospitalizations in 133 cities in Brazil. Almost a third of the study population reported at least one previous COVID-19 case, with wealthier individuals presenting more COVID-19 episodes, whereas those with more than one episode were more likely to be hospitalized, along with individuals with comorbidities. Our findings provide valuable insights into the regional disparities of the COVID-19 burden in Brazil and the relationship between socioeconomic factors and COVID-19 prevalence in the country.
