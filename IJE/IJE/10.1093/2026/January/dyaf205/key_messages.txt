We assessed susceptible childhood windows for nitrogen dioxide (NO 2 ), ozone, and particulate matter ≤10 µm in diameter exposure associated with self-reported adult bronchitic symptoms. We found evidence that early life (ages 1–2 years) is a susceptible window for NO 2 exposure. There was no evidence of differences by sex. Our findings add unique knowledge of susceptible windows for childhood exposure to air pollution for adult respiratory diseases.
