The UK Health Security Agency developed a suite of forecasting models for COVID-19, influenza, and RSV hospital admissions and bed occupancy used in real time over the winter 2023–4 season. The forecasts accurately predicted over 14-day horizons, particularly at subnational levels, and for influenza and RSV targets. Respiratory pathogen hospital burden forecasts allow improved resource allocation in healthcare systems, which we demonstrated through close collaboration between healthcare operations and public health modelers.
