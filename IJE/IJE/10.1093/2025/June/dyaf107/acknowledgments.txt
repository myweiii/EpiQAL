At the request of the UK Committee on the Medical Aspects of Radiation in the Environment (COMARE), the UK Small Area Health Statistics Unit (SAHSU), Imperial College London, has undertaken surveillance of childhood cancer in the vicinity of UK nuclear power plants and installations. Cancer data for England were supplied by Public Health England (now UK Health Security Agency). Approval for data dissemination was sought from the Caldicott Advisory Panel under the auspices of the Public Health England Caldicott Guardian under Regulation 3 of The Health Service (Control of Patient Information) Regulations 2002: Ref number: CGRP2017_14_SAHSU. Cancer data for Wales, were supplied by the Welsh Cancer Intelligence and Surveillance Unit. Cancer data for Scotland (Scottish cancer registrations) were supplied by Public Health Scotland following approval obtained from the NHS Scotland Public Benefit and Privacy Panel for Health and Social Care (Ref number: 1819–0019). We thank Weiyi Wang for assistance with drafting figures.
