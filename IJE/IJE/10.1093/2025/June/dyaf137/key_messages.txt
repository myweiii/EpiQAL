We investigated whether analgesic opioid use during pregnancy is associated with increased risks of placental abruption, pre-eclampsia, preterm birth, and fetal growth restriction (FGR). Opioid use in pregnancy was associated with modestly increased risks of placental abruption and preterm birth, particularly with codeine and oxycodone exposure, but not with pre-eclampsia or FGR. These findings inform clinicians and patients about specific pregnancy risks related to opioid analgesic use, aiding safer prescribing and risk counselling during pregnancy.
