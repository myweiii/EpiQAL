We analysed the impact of COVID-19 containment policies on health services for major non-communicable diseases (NCDs), including cardiovascular disease (CVD), respiratory disease, cancer, kidney disease, and hypertension (HTN), by examining outpatient department visits and the use of free health services during the pandemic. Hospital visits for CVD and cancer were notably affected, while conclusive evidence of the impact was not detected for HTN, chronic respiratory disease, or kidney disease. Interruption in essential health services during public health emergencies can lead to delayed diagnoses and suboptimal treatment, highlighting the importance of ensuring continuous care for NCD patients.
