Text message reminders have been reported to improve the completeness and timeliness of administering birth dose vaccines—Bacillus Calmette-Guerin (BCG) and oral polio vaccine (OPV)—however, evidence of effectiveness in an informal settlement is lacking. In a stepped-wedge cluster-randomized trial, we found text message reminders tended toward improving the timely administration of birth dose vaccines—administration of birth dose vaccines at birth or within 2 weeks of birth—among newborns in an urban informal settlement in Nairobi County, Kenya. Text message reminders could be a useful tool for improving vaccination coverage in urban informal settlements in Kenya and similar settings.
