The application of lipoprotein(a) [Lp(a)]-lowering treatment for cardiovascular events, as well as its potential interactions with other treatments like low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C)-lowering therapy and lifestyle improvements, remains uncertain. A sub-additive interaction between Lp(a) lowering and LDL-C lowering in reducing the risk of peripheral artery disease (PAD) was observed; however, no clear evidence that the effect of varying Lp(a) in isolation will differ from the effect of varying Lp(a) in conjunction with lifestyle interventions. Combination therapy is not recommended for Lp(a)-lowering treatment in reducing cardiovascular risk.
