There is a significant overlap in symptoms between malaria and pneumonia, which can lead to the misdiagnosis of both conditions. In malaria-endemic settings in sub-Saharan Africa, as many as one in four cases of clinical pneumonia, as defined by the World Health Organization, is misdiagnosed due to malaria. Certain clinical features such as lower chest wall indrawing, nasal flaring, and crackles on chest auscultation indicate pneumonia in children with malaria parasitaemia.
