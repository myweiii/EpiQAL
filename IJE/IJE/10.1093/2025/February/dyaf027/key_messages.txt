We investigated how extreme temperatures were associated with hospitalization, revealing double disparities between Medical Aid (MA) and non-MA populations and within MA beneficiaries. The cold and heat risks were higher in MA individuals than in non-MA individuals, and the risk differences were generally more prominent in young and middle-aged (aged 0–64 years) populations, hospitalizations for cardiovascular and mental disorders, and people with disabilities than in the total population. This study highlights the double disparities among MA beneficiaries, providing key insights for targeted public health interventions.
