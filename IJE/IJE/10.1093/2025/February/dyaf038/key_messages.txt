Our article forecast smoking rates in Australia across multiple sociodemographic groups simultaneously, to assess likely achievement of the country’s commercial tobacco endgame goal of ≤5% daily smoking prevalence by 2030. Using an innovative simulated annealing approach to fill existing data gaps in historic smoking prevalence in Australia by sociodemographic strata, we found that the 2030 endgame goal is unlikely to be achieved and is particularly out of reach for those population groups currently experiencing the greatest smoking burden under the force of current trends. Forecasted smoking rates across sociodemographic groups produced by our model can support planning towards an equitable tobacco endgame being achieved in Australia, in addition to being replicable in other jurisdictions wanting to gain a better understanding of smoking trends.
