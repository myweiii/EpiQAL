We would like to express our gratitude to the people with lived experience of leprosy who have shaped our understanding of the condition. We acknowledge that some information used in this work was obtained through observations of people affected by leprosy who may not have been allowed agency to object to being included in the work in which their information was included. We believe their important contribution should be acknowledged.
